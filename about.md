## Rob Koch

I'm an [AWS Data Hero](https://builder.aws.com) and cloud/data technology consultant at [Slalom](https://www.slalom.com) in Seattle, working across AWS architecture, data platforms, and Kubernetes. I was named to the [Redgate 100](https://www.red-gate.com) in 2022, and I'm the author of Manning's [Python and Kafka liveProject](https://www.manning.com/liveproject/python-and-kafka).

I speak at conferences, corporate events, and professional summits on AI-assisted work, data platforms, and accessibility in tech — including a [keynote at KubeCon Europe 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UfYctUtDDfQ). I also co-chair the [CNCF Deaf and Hard of Hearing Working Group](https://contribute.cncf.io), working to make cloud-native tech more accessible.
